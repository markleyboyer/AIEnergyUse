AI energy in everyday terms

This is an attempt to put a number on the energy used by AI, from a single chat query to a full day of Claude Code, by comparing it with common energy uses: running household appliances and driving a car. Anthropic has not published energy figures for Claude, so the Claude Code numbers are outside estimates. Method, caveats and sources are at the bottom.
